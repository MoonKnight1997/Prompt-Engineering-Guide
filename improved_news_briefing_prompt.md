- You MUST cover ALL 5 topics listed below, in order

- You MUST research current news from the last 24 hours only
- If you cannot find enough stories for a topic, state this explicitly rather than skip it

1. **UK News and Politics** - Minimum 3 stories

## PHASE 1: Research and Verification

Before writing, systematically research each topic:

### Research Checklist
For each of the 5 topics above:
- [ ] Search for the most significant news from the last 24 hours
- [ ] Identify at least the minimum number of stories required
- [ ] Verify key facts (dates, figures, names, locations) against ≥2 authoritative sources (BBC, Reuters, AP, FT, Bloomberg, etc.)
- [ ] Note any overlapping stories that might fit multiple topics (assign to the MOST relevant topic only)
- [ ] For uncertain information, note hedging language needed ("reportedly", "sources indicate", "according to")

### Verification Standards
- Cross-check all quantitative data (numbers, percentages, currency amounts)
- Verify proper attribution of quotes and statements
- Confirm timeline and chronological accuracy
- Identify and separate facts from opinions
- Reject unverified claims from social media/blogs
- Ensure multiple viewpoints are captured where relevant

## Writing Standards

**Language and Style:**
- Use UK English spelling (organisation, realised, etc.)
- Use correct currency symbols in context (£ for UK, $ for US, € for EU)
- Attribute officials and statements to correct jurisdictions (UK Government, US Administration, etc.)
- Write in clear, concise, professional tone suitable for an executive briefing

**Accuracy and Attribution:**
- Clearly differentiate facts from opinions - attribute all opinions to their sources
- Use hedging language for uncertain developments ("reportedly", "sources indicate", "according to officials")
- Maintain chronological accuracy in timelines
- Reflect source material faithfully through accurate quotes or paraphrasing

**Formatting:**
- **Bold** important names, figures, and key statistics for scannability
- Keep each story to one focused paragraph
- Use clear, descriptive story titles
- Ensure stories appear in only ONE topic section (no duplication)

**De-duplication Rule:**
If a story could fit multiple topics (e.g., UK tech policy could be UK News OR Technology), assign it to the MOST relevant topic only. Priority order for ambiguous stories:
1. If it's primarily about UK/US government action → Politics sections
2. If it's primarily about technology/AI developments → Technology section
3. If it's primarily about markets/economic indicators → Finance section
4. If it's about international relations/foreign events → World News
